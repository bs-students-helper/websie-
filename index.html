FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about BSH student resources and tools.

Is BSH completely free for IIT Madras BS students?

Yes, 100%! BSH (BS Students Helper) is an open community project built by students to support fellow peers in the IITM BS degree. All practice quizzes, PYQ archives, and SEC calculators are completely free.

How accurate is the SEC Grade Predictor?

Our predictor uses the exact grading formulas published in the official IITM BS course handbook (including best 10 of 12 weekly assignment weightages, Quiz 1, Quiz 2, and End-Term combinations).

Which subjects are currently covered in the Quiz Engine?

We currently cover all Foundational level courses (Maths 1 & 2, Statistics 1 & 2, Computational Thinking, English 1 & 2, Python Programming) as well as core Diploma in Data Science & Electronic Systems courses.

How can I contribute notes or report a question mistake?

You can join our community Discord server or click the "Add Your Review & Feedback" button above to submit course notes, PYQ solutions, or flag question corrections.
